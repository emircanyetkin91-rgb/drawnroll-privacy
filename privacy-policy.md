# DrawnRoll Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 13, 2026**

DrawnRoll respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the DrawnRoll mobile game.

## Information Stored on Your Device

DrawnRoll may store gameplay information locally on your device, including:

- Best distance and score history
- Game settings
- Selected ball/customization preferences
- Locally created custom ball images

This information is used only to provide and maintain game functionality.

## Advertising

DrawnRoll uses Google AdMob to display rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

- Device identifiers
- Advertising-related data
- Basic device and app information
- Ad interactions

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and measurement purposes in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

Where required, DrawnRoll requests your advertising consent through Google's consent management system before ads are initialized.

DrawnRoll does not require you to watch rewarded advertisements unless you choose to use the related optional in-game benefit.

## Photos

DrawnRoll may request permission to save an image to your photo library when you explicitly choose a feature such as **Save Image**.

The app does not access your photo library for unrelated purposes.

## Analytics

Advertising services may process limited analytics information related to ad delivery and interaction. DrawnRoll does not currently operate a separate user profiling or account-based analytics system.

## Accounts and Personal Information

DrawnRoll does not require an account and does not directly request information such as your name, address, phone number, or password.

## Data Sharing

DrawnRoll does not sell your personal information.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob is handled according to those providers' own privacy policies and applicable consent choices.

## Your Privacy Choices

Where available, you can review or update advertising privacy choices from the **Settings** section of DrawnRoll.

You may also reset or limit advertising identifiers through your device's privacy settings.

## Data Retention and Deletion

DrawnRoll stores gameplay data locally on your device.

You can remove locally stored DrawnRoll data at any time by deleting the app.

Advertising-related data handled by Google AdMob is retained and deleted according to Google's own retention and deletion policies.
